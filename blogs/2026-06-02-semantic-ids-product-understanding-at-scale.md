Key Contributors: Shrikar Archak, Karuna Ahuja, Soroush Sobhkhiz, Marko Avdalovic, Xiyu Wang, JiChao Zhang, Hao Yan, Chris Hartley Introduction Operating a grocery catalog at Instacart’s scale means managing millions of products across thousands of categories. Every product is assigned to a category in our hierarchical taxonomy like “Dairy > Cheese > Parmesan”. These categories provide broad classification, but they miss the connections that drive how customers actually shop.
