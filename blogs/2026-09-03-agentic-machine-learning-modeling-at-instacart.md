Tilman Drerup, Moe Moazzami, Shih-Ting Lin, Greg Reda (and many more) Introduction At Instacart, artificial intelligence is fundamentally changing the way our machine learning engineers operate. The post below goes a few levels deeper and zooms in on the machine learning modeling process itself, an area where recent developments in AI-assisted research have opened up exciting new frontiers that we are now actively exploring.
