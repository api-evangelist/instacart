TL;DR Background: Marketing Across Marketplace and Storefront Instacart operates across two distinct commerce experiences: Instacart Marketplace, our first-party consumer marketplace Storefront Pro, our white-label e-commerce platform for retailers For years, our marketing automation infrastructure was built primarily to support Marketplace use cases. That model worked well in a first-party environment, where the product experience, customer relationship, and brand were all centrally managed by Instacart. Storefront Pro introduced a very different set of requirements.
